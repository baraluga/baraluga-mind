## Summary

# AI's Impact on Junior Development and Career Paths

- Core tension: AI handles part of the output that historically also developed junior talent
  - Output production and people-growing have always been bundled together
  - AI decouples them, and no one yet knows how to replace the developmental function
- Key unknowns Dimitri acknowledged openly:
  - No timeline on structural change
  - No visibility into which new roles will emerge in 3 years
  - No clear model yet for how juniors build judgment without repetitive foundational work
- Junior retention argument: full-career journeys at Engie produce far lower turnover than external hires
  - Commitment to preserve junior career paths regardless of AI adoption

# Mental Health, Isolation, and Well-Being Concerns

- Employees raising anxiety on two fronts:
  - Loss of purpose: "What's my role if AI produces what I used to produce?"
  - Responsibility creep: output multiplied by AI, but accountability stays with the human
    - Manageable at 2-3x; potentially unsustainable at 10-50x
- Risk of isolation flagged: AI answering questions that colleagues used to answer
  - Fewer peer interactions, more "teams of one"
  - Dimitri's response: go to the office, create team moments deliberately
- Psychological support already available:
  - Employee Assistance Program (varies by country), 24/7 line, multiple psychologist sessions
  - Links on HR website and in the Engagement Meter under country resources
- Mental health impact confirmed as a concern at senior leadership level

# Commitments, Training, and the Ecological Question

- Dimitri's explicit commitments to the team:
  - No surprises on structural changes: direct communication before any announcement
  - Productivity gains go to backlog and additional tasks before any headcount reduction
  - Ongoing 1:1 and group conversations available
- Training approach in place:
  - Individual objective set for all N-1: integrate GenAI into their own ways of working
  - GitHub Copilot training pushed to everyone; open to anyone who hasn't done it yet
  - AI Venturers program available for broader GenAI upskilling
- No formal change management or reskilling program exists yet
  - HR is mapping GenAI impact by job family (high/medium/low) but still at early reflection stage
- Ecological concern raised: AI energy consumption vs. Engie's net-zero commitment
  - Carbon budget pushed down to entity P&L level to drive decisions closer to the ground
  - Token and energy consumption tracked; net-zero targets (2040/2050) remain in force
- Dimitri's personal note: his speech was written by hand, not generated by AI
  - Message: use AI for brain-heavy tasks; don't use it for everything
  - "You are the driver, you have a super fast engine. You are still the driver."
