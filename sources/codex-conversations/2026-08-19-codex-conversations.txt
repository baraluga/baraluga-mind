Pinakasimple explanation: an Airflow Asset is a named “this data is ready” signal.

Instead of:

```text
Run consumer DAG every day at 10:00
and hope the producer finished by 09:55
```

you get:

```text
Producer successfully publishes dataset
→ marks the Asset updated
→ Airflow starts the consumer
```

Airflow also shows which DAG produces and consumes the Asset. It marks the Asset updated only when the producing task succeeds. [Apache Airflow asset scheduling](https://airflow.apache.org/docs/apache-airflow/3.1.5/authoring-and-scheduling/asset-scheduling.html)

→ capacity Asset updated
→ reconciliation DAG starts
