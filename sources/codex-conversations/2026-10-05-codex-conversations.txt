# Send Grafana alerts to a Microsoft Teams channel

**TL;DR:** Teams channels have their own email address. Point a Grafana Email contact point at it and alerts show up as posts in the channel. No webhooks, no Power Automate, no admin approval.

This page covers the Teams side and the contact-point wiring. For creating alert rules and the rest of the Grafana email setup, see [Grafana email alerts setup guide – link TBD].

**Who does this:** the people who want the alerts (traders, analysts, etc.), not the SMP team. We provide Grafana and working SMTP delivery. The channel, the recipients and the alert rules are yours.

**Scope:** verified end-to-end on India QA (firing notification arrived in a test channel). Other instances need their own rollout first; check with the SMP team that email delivery is enabled for yours.

## Before you start

- A Teams channel to receive alerts. **Use a dedicated one.** Don't reuse the Airflow failure channel (`Scraper Notification`), or the two streams will bury each other.
- Edit access to Grafana Alerting (Editor or above). Viewers can't create contact points.

## 1. Get the channel's email address

1. In Teams, find your channel, click the `...` next to its name, and choose **Get email address**.
2. Click **Copy**. It looks like `1fd93051.online.engie.com@emea.teams.ms`.
3. Copy only the address. If you see something like `Name <address>`, drop the display name and the angle brackets.

## 2. Allow the channel to accept email from Grafana

This is the step everyone misses. By default the channel drops emails from senders it doesn't know, **silently**. No error, no bounce, no post.

1. In the same dialog, open **Advanced settings**.
2. Choose **Anyone can send emails to this email address**.
3. Save.

> **Read this.** "Anyone" means anyone who has the address can post to your channel. Treat the address like a password: don't paste it in public channels or tickets, and don't reuse a channel that has sensitive content. If you can restrict senders to the `engie.com` domain and delivery still works, prefer that.

## 3. Add it as a Grafana contact point

1. In Grafana, go to **Alerting → Contact points → Add contact point**.
2. Name it something you'll recognise, e.g. `Teams - <your team> alerts`.
3. Set **Integration** to **Email**.
4. Paste the channel address into **Addresses**. Only the Teams address, unless you want a copy in someone's inbox too (separate multiple with `,` or `;`).
5. Save, then press **Test**. A post should appear in the channel within a minute or two.

Nothing showing up? Check, in order: the address has no display name attached, step 2 is saved, and a plain email from your own mailbox to the same address posts. If your own email posts but Grafana's doesn't, tell the SMP team.

## 4. Route your alerts to it

A contact point on its own does nothing. Alerts only reach it if a notification policy or the rule itself points there. Follow [the alert rule and routing steps in the setup guide – link TBD], and select your new contact point.

## 5. Make the post readable

Expect this: the Teams post shows the email **subject**, not the body. The full Grafana email (labels, values, buttons) is behind **View original email**. So the subject has to carry the information.

Set a custom subject on the contact point under **Optional Email settings → Subject**:

    {{ .Status | toUpper }}: {{ .CommonLabels.alertname }} - {{ .CommonAnnotations.summary }}

Then fill in the **Summary** annotation on each alert rule with something a human can act on. Rule annotations use `$labels` / `$values`, not `.CommonLabels`, so keep that field plain text or `{{ $labels.<name> }}`.

> Don't put the subject template in the alert rule's Summary field. It's a different template scope and will not render.

## What you'll see

| Event | Teams post |
| --- | --- |
| Alert fires | `[FIRING:1] <alert name>` (or your custom subject) |
| Alert recovers | `[RESOLVED] <alert name>` |

The **Test** button only sends a firing-style message. It never produces a resolved one, so don't judge recovery from it.

## Limitations

- **No pings.** Email-to-channel posts don't @mention anyone. Members get notified only if they follow the channel, so tell your team to turn channel notifications on.
- **Body is collapsed.** See step 5.
- **One address, one channel.** A different audience means another channel and another contact point.
- **Airflow failures are separate.** Those go to their own channel and are not affected by anything on this page.
