**Your question:** no, collection doesn't use TSDB's frequency. It's one central rule for every asset and tag:
- **Collection (the collector):** it polls every 15 seconds (`DARWIN_POLL_SECONDS=15`) and keeps the first valid reading of each source minute (`first_per_minute`). Every stream ends up with one sample per minute, whatever it publishes to.
- **Publishing (the Airflow publisher):** `mappings.json` decides which outputs each stream gets.
  - If a row has a `minute_series_id`, its minute samples are written as-is to the PT1M series. Only `ActivePower` has one.
  - Every row has a `quarter_hour_series_id`, which gets the **average of that quarter-hour's minute samples** in the PT15M series.

So TSDB's PT1M/PT15M is reflected in `mappings.json` (entered by hand, and checked by the probe we just ran), not read at runtime.

I wouldn't make collection follow TSDB's frequency either. The PT15M series are averages, so they need the minute samples. Sampling once every 15 minutes would give a single snapshot instead of an average. Reading frequencies from TSDB at runtime would also add a network dependency for something that never changes.
