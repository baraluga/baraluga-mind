## 11:44 assistant

It's almost certainly an Okta group membership issue, not something you fix by clicking around in Grafana.

**Why prod works but QA doesn't**

Every Grafana (dev, qa and prod, for both India and Japan) sets the user's role from their Okta groups each time they sign in ([india/qa/grafana_values.yml:75](smp-dashboard/configs/helm/india/qa/grafana_values.yml:75)):

- `smp-dashboard-admin` → GrafanaAdmin
- `smp-dashboard-editor` → Admin, which is the role that can create contact points
- anything else → Viewer

The catch is that **QA and dev sign in through a different Okta tenant**:
- Prod uses `gem.okta-emea.com`.
- QA uses `gem-beta.oktapreview.com`, the preview tenant.

The group lists in the two tenants are separate. Lou is probably in `smp-dashboard-editor` in prod Okta but not in the preview tenant, so QA gives them Viewer.

**The fix:** add Lou to `smp-dashboard-editor` in the **preview** Okta tenant. Lou then needs to sign out of QA Grafana and sign back in, because the role is only re-read at login.

Two things to avoid:
- **Don't change Lou's role in Grafana's user admin.** The setting `skip_org_role_sync: false` means Okta overwrites it at Lou's next login.
- **Add them to `-editor`, not `-admin`.** `-admin` makes them a server-wide Grafana admin, which is more than they need.

**How to check this yourself:** in QA Grafana, go to Administration → Users, find Lou and look at their role. If it says Viewer, this is the cause.
