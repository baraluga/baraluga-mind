Darwin only keeps 24 h of history (10-min steps), so I couldn't check whether this happened before.

**Raganesda: whole site cut off at once**
- Every device I sampled has its last point at exactly 19 Oct, 14:20 UTC (19:50 IST). That covers 100 of 1,252 series: inverters, the grid meter and the irradiance sensor.
- The inverters' last values are all `0.0`, as you'd expect at night. Then nothing more arrived, including at sunrise.
- The other solar sites (Khavda) have all 144 points over the same 24 h.
- So the site's link to Darwin went down at dusk and never came back. This isn't a night-mode pause.

**Tithwa: drop-off, not a single cut**
- 11 of its 14 series (farm plus turbines) stopped at 02:40 UTC. The others stopped at 02:50, 03:20 and 04:40.
- The farm's final point (02:50) is exactly `0.0` for both power and wind speed. That's a placeholder written when the link drops, not a real reading.
- Before that, wind was about 2.5 m/s, so the turbines were idle but still reporting.

**Is it expected?** No for both, unless these sites routinely have gaps, and I can't verify that. My best guess is site comms failures that nobody is fixing because of the holiday, with Raganesda on day 2 and Tithwa since this morning.
